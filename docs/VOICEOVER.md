# Nims demo: voice-over script

For `nims-demo.mp4` (about 84 s). Around 190 words, so a calm pace of roughly 140 words a minute fits. The timecodes are estimates taken from the recording script: check them against the video and nudge a line if it drifts.

| Time | On screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Title card | Meet Nims: a market town where every trader is an AI agent, with its own cash, its own stock, and its own mind about prices. |
| 0:05 | Email sign-in | You sign in with an email. That's it. No wallet, no seed phrase, no gas. |
| 0:14 | Goal picker | Pick a goal, and Nim, your guide, always tells you the next step. |
| 0:20 | Collecting copper wire, then Anil's stall; price tooltip | Collect goods at wholesale, then carry them to a stall that pays more. Hover any price and the merchant explains it in their own words: how much stock they hold, who sold to them this hour, what they can afford. |
| 0:32 | The sale | Sell, and your cash moves instantly. |
| 0:36 | Delivery order form, then the filled order | Too far to walk? Place a delivery order. Name the goods and the lowest price you'll take. The town's carriers wait until the stall pays it, take the load over, and sell it for you. |
| 0:53 | Price board | The price board shows every good in every market, coloured against its usual price. Flood one stall and you'll see it go red. |
| 0:58 | Mira's stall, shares | You can own a piece of a merchant and earn a share of their profit each evening. |
| 1:03 | Fast-forward page | For judges: fast-forward a whole week of the market in seconds. It uses the same pricing code as the live game. |
| 1:15 | The chart, then the outro | One trader's behaviour moves the whole market, with no developer patch. That's the economy as the gameplay. Underneath, every good and coin settles to NEAR testnet, and you never notice. |

## Reading notes
- Keep "you never notice" as the last beat. It is the bonus the track scores.
- Don't read the numbers on screen (prices, balances); they change on each recording.
- The video shows no chain scene. If someone asks, the answer is in the README: settlement runs in the background and each player has a proof link in the News panel.
