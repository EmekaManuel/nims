Nims privacy policy

Last updated 6 November 2026

Nims is a hackathon game about a market town. It runs on the NEAR testnet: nothing in it has real-world money value. This page says what the game keeps about you and why.

What we collect

  • Your email address. You give it when you sign in with an emailed link or with Google. With Google we receive only your email address, and only if Google has verified it. We never see your Google password, contacts or any other Google data.
  • Your game progress: your coins, goods, shares, stalls and the actions you take in the game.
  • A game account on the NEAR testnet, created for you automatically. Its name is derived from your email in a way that does not reveal it. The key is stored encrypted on our server. You never handle it.
  • A sign-in cookie that keeps you signed in for up to 30 days, and a short-lived cookie used only while signing in with Google.

How we use it

Only to run the game: to recognise you when you come back, to keep your progress, and to settle the game's trades on the testnet. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use it for tracking.

Other services

  • Google handles the "Continue with Google" sign-in.
  • NEAR AI Cloud writes some of the merchants' lines. It receives game facts such as an item and a price, not your email or identity.
  • ElevenLabs turns those lines into speech. It receives the line of text, not your identity.
  • The NEAR testnet is a public blockchain. Game activity written to it is public and cannot be deleted, but it is tied to an opaque game account name, not to your email.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your account and progress for as long as the game is running. To have your email and progress deleted, write to the address below and we will remove them. Testnet records that are already on the public chain cannot be removed.
